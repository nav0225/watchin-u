# Data report: session 2026-11-04 (first real footage)

**Source:** `2026-11-04-dataset.zip`. Nine Hikvision web-client recordings of Camera 01, main
stream: 2560×1440, H.264 Main, 20 fps, 3.0–5.8 Mbit/s. Recorded 10:15–12:34 IST, clips of
12–105 s, about 6.5 minutes in total.

## 1. The camera view is fixed

Static structure (shed, trusses) in every clip was matched against the first clip, with ORB features and
a RANSAC similarity transform, and OSD overlays masked. Every clip aligns to within
**0.4 px at scale 1.000**. The PTZ is locked, and differences between clips are only where
trucks park. One calibration therefore serves all of them.

## 4. Implications acted on

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| Two lanes in one view, busy at once | Two calibrated bays; per-lane state machines and decisions |
| Lanes overlap in perspective (near-lane coil tops reach into the far lane) | Detector runs once on the full frame; coils assigned to a lane by box centre |
| Ingot trucks are tarped too | Product gate (`wire_rod` vs `other_load`) before anything heavy runs; tarps are not evidence of wire rods |
| Yellow **and** black tarps | Both in the stage training data; collect more of each |
| Same frame, different truck per lane | Stage and layout labels are per lane; classifier export makes one crop per lane |
| Fixed camera: whole-frame dHash collapsed 365 of 393 frames | Change-based de-duplication (per-region change, 3 s / 10 s gaps): 86 of 393 frames kept |
| Burned-in clock changes every second | OSD masks calibrated; blanked in de-duplication, training exports and runtime |
| Busy day = one continuous visit group | Leakage-safe split can't split one session; `--group-by clip` bootstrap for v0 only |
| Frames are ~1.7 MB (JPEG q95) / ~1.0 MB (`-q:v 2`) | Time-lapse storage re-measured: ~0.36 GB/h at 10 s vs ~2.5 GB/h video |

## 5. Calibration

* **Far lane:** homography fitted to the 10 coil centres of V1 (2×5). Mean residual 7 px and max
  23 px at 1600×900. All 10 slot centres fall on the coils, and on V3's coils as well.
* **Near lane:** fitted to V4's 4 visible rows. The 5th (rear) row is predicted 1.45 frame heights
  down, outside the image, so it is reported as `out_of_view`, not empty.

## 6b. Bootstrap pre-labels and a first count check

`watchinu prelabel --method wrap-colour` on the 86 kept frames gave 1,141 boxes (coils and
workers) in 2 min 40 s on CPU. `watchinu label-audit` against the draft visit log:

| Truck / lane | Truth | Pre-labelled coils per frame |
|---|---|---|
| V1 far, linear, all visible | 10 | **median 10** (8–11) |
| V3 far, criss-cross | ~14 | median 15 (14–16) while open; 8–9 when workers or the near truck block it |
| V4 near | 8 visible (rear row out of view) | 7.5–10 |
| Ingot lane V2, tarped trucks | 0 | 0 |
| Empty near lane | 0 | 0–1 (false positive, probably V1's white-and-blue cabin) |

So with **zero human labels**, the calibration, lane assignment and wrap-colour pre-labeller
already count a fully visible linear load correctly and keep ingots and tarps out.
Remaining errors are typical: occasional duplicates (V3), boxes spanning two coils, the
odd blue-and-white non-coil.

**Bug found and fixed on the way:** `prelabel` applied the trained-detector default
`--min-conf 0.15` to the bootstrap proposals, whose scores are meaningless vocabulary
scores. That silently dropped 40% of the coils (678 vs 1,141 boxes). The default is now
per method. Colour statistics are also computed on quarter-scale polygons: 3.9× faster,
same coils.
